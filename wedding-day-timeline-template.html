Photo blocks run long, and one late block makes every number after it wrong too.

Four opinions on filling it in

  1. (Opinion.) Pad the gaps around photo blocks. Group photos run long because relatives wander off between groupings. A written 15-minute buffer between photo blocks absorbs that without pushing the ceremony.
  2. (Opinion.) Fill the fixed commitments first, then the soft slots. Vendor arrivals, the ceremony start, and dinner service are the anchors. Everything else bends around them, and the reverse order doesn't hold.
  3. (Opinion.) Hand the sheet to someone with no jobs that day. A friend who isn't getting dressed, photographed, or toasting is the only person free to watch the clock and nudge people along.
  4. (Opinion.) Write times in pencil. The sheet changes twice before the week of, which is why the planner's week-of list starts with "Print the day-of timeline for the wedding party". Reprint the final version then.
