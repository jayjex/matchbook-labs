Worksheet 1 — Letter Tracing: Aa, Bb, Cc

Trace the gray letters, then write the letter twice on the line. Big to small, top to bottom.

A A A A

a a a a

B B B B

b b b b

C C c c

Answer + stroke steps (tap to reveal)

A: 1) slant down-left, 2) slant down-right from the same top point, 3) bar across the middle. a: 1) circle, 2) short line down the right side. B: 1) line straight down, 2) bump out and back to the middle, 3) second bump out and back to the bottom. b: 1) line straight down, 2) bump out to the right at the bottom. C: 1) start at the top right, curve left and around, open to the right. c: same shape, smaller.

Worksheet 2 — Count and Circle

Count the shapes. Circle the number that matches.

● ● ● ● ● ●

468

Answer (tap to reveal)

6. Count each dot once, touching it as you say the number; touching keeps the count from skipping or doubling.

Worksheet 3 — CVC Word Build: C _ T

Build the word. The picture is a cat, the first and last letters are placed. Pick the middle letter from the tiles.

CT

Tiles: a i o u

Answer + how to help (tap to reveal)

a, the word is cat. Say the word slowly and stretch the middle sound: c-aaaa-t. The stretched sound is the vowel that goes in the box. Testing the tiles in order (can it be cit? cot?) also works, and hearing which one is a real word is itself good practice.

Worksheet 4 — What Comes Next?

Find the pattern. Which shape comes in the empty box?

▲ ● ▲ ● ▲ ?

▲●■

Answer + reasoning (tap to reveal)

● (circle). The row alternates triangle, circle, triangle, circle. After a triangle comes a circle, so the box gets a circle. Say the pattern out loud ("triangle, circle, triangle, circle") and the missing shape becomes obvious.
