Web3 Builder OS is a Notion template for builders shipping across several chains. Five linked databases and one dashboard keep projects, funding, content, learning, and contacts in a single workspace, so "what am I working on" stops being a scroll through ten apps.

The details

What you get:

How it ships: a Notion-ready bundle of markdown files plus 5 import-ready CSVs and a setup guide. Import the CSVs, paste the pages, add 3 relations by drag-and-drop. Under 10 minutes, click by click. Works on the free Notion plan. No API integration, no third-party app, nothing to renew.

The example rows are real builder situations (a Base paymaster demo, a DoraHacks hackathon submission, a Cairo book study log), so you see the system working before you swap in your own work. Status definitions, view filters, and weekly review steps are all written out. Duplicate nothing, configure once, update as you go.

Educational productivity template. Not financial advice, not affiliated with Notion or any chain or platform named in the example data.
