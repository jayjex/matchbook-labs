No claims here about how motivation works. This is a place to write things down and check them off, nothing more.

Four opinions on filling it in

  1. (Opinion.) Three goals max. Six action rows split across four or more goals leaves barely one action per goal, which is a list of wishes. Three goals and two solid actions each is a week with room to breathe.
  2. (Opinion.) Write actions a stranger could grade. The 14-page pack's rule sheet asks for a number, a duration, a yes or a no. The same test works here: "move more" can't fail, so it can't pass either. "30 minutes, Monday to Friday" can do both.
  3. (Opinion.) Run the review on the same weekday, ten minutes. Make one change at a time. Three adjustments in one review means next week's sheet is three experiments at once, and none of them gets a fair read.
  4. (Opinion.) Print two copies. One on the desk, one spare. A sheet filled in badly beats a neat one filled in never, and a week that marks three of six actions is still a week with numbers on it. Reprint fresh every week.
