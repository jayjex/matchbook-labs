Worksheet 2 — Trace Numbers 1, 2, 3

Stroke order (tap to reveal)

1: straight line down, done. 2: curve up and over to the left, slant down to the bottom, line across. 3: two bumps — start top left, curve out and back to the middle, then a second curve out and back at the bottom. Backward 3s are normal at this age and usually sort themselves out with practice.

Four Tips for Worksheet Sessions

  1. Keep it short. Five to ten minutes, then stop even if the page is half done. Stopping while it still feels like a game is what gets them back to the table tomorrow.
  2. Count real things before paper. Snacks, stairs, toy cars, socks. The symbols on the page make sense after a kid has counted things they can actually grab.
  3. Skip the flat "no" on wrong answers. "Let's count that one again together" keeps them in the game and shows you where the count went sideways.
  4. Chunky crayon beats pencil. Small hands hold fat crayons better, and tracing works fine in crayon. Print at 100% scale so the numbers stay big.
