Fair Market Rent by ZIP Code — Free FY2026 Sample

This is real data from HUD's FY2026 Fair Market Rent release: 20 rows, one ZIP per major metro area, monthly rent for 0- through 4-bedroom units. Use it to sanity-check a listing price or compare metros. The full dataset behind this table covers 51,895 ZIP codes and is linked below.

What FMR means: Fair Market Rent is HUD's estimate of the 40th percentile gross rent (shelter rent plus utilities) for standard-quality units in a local market. Housing agencies use it to set payment standards for the Housing Choice Voucher program. Amounts below are monthly dollars, exactly as published. For the full dataset behind these rows, see the HUD fair market rent data pack — 51,895 ZIP rows plus county and state rollups as clean CSV.

What's in the Full Dataset

  • fmr-by-zip-2026.csv — 51,895 rows, one per ZIP code: FMR for 0-4 bedrooms, HUD area code, metro flag, area name, state.
  • fmr-by-county-2026.csv — 3,229 counties with median and max FMR per bedroom size, plus an area_rows count showing how many HUD areas were rolled up.
  • state-summary.csv — 52 rows (50 states + DC + PR) for quick state-level comparisons.
  • Data dictionary + README — every column explained, including how SAFMRs work and where HUD's 90%/110% payment standards fit.
  • Clean CSV, no merged cells or footnotes — joins straight onto anything with a ZIP or county column.

Typical uses: pricing a rental against the local 40th percentile, tenant screening ranges, rent comparator models, market research, and dashboard backends. The data is public domain, so what you pay for is the parsing and cleanup, not a license.
