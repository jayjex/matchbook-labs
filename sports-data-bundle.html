Check the Zip Before You Trust It

Every bundle ships with SHA256SUMS.txt, one SHA-256 hash per component zip. For a dataset this matters in a concrete way: a truncated download or a silently swapped file produces the same symptom, numbers that look fine until your model doesn't. A checksum is a fingerprint of the exact bytes. If sha256sum -c prints OK on all four lines, the files on your disk are the files that were built, byte for byte. We ran that check on a fresh extract before packaging; run it yourself after download:

sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt

CC BY 4.0, in Plain Terms

Two of the four packs are datasets built on CC BY 4.0 sources: NFL game data from nflverse, Airbnb listings from Inside Airbnb. CC BY 4.0 lets you copy, remix, republish and build commercial work on the data. The single condition is credit. If you republish, include "Data from nflverse, CC BY 4.0" and "Data by Inside Airbnb, InsideAirbnb.com, CC BY 4.0". Both credit lines ship inside the zips in ATTRIBUTION.txt, so pasting them into your project takes seconds. The rent data is a US federal government work (HUD FY2026 Fair Market Rent), public domain under 17 USC 105, with no attribution required. The wallpapers are original images: personal use plus commercial use inside your own work, but don't resell or re-upload the pack as-is.
