How snowball order works, in 4 steps

  1. List every debt with its balance and minimum payment, copied from current statements.
  2. Number them smallest balance first. Smallest balance = #1. That numbering is the snowball order.
  3. Pay the minimum on every debt, and send every dollar above the minimums to debt #1.
  4. When #1 is paid off, roll its whole payment into #2. The payment for a finished debt moves to the next one, so the amount going to debt stays the same while the number of debts shrinks.

That is the whole mechanism. Which order to pick, and what it costs in interest, is covered with real numbers on the free debt snowball worksheet (snowball vs avalanche). This page stays on the tracking side.
