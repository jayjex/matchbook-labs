How to use this hub: start at the order below and work down. Close out the year you are in first, then decide the shape of next year, then put January on paper, then choose the weekly pages that carry it. Skip any step you have already done.

The order that works

Four steps, in this order. Step three is where plans fall over when step one never happened.

  1. Close the year you are in. The yearly review worksheet gets wins, lessons and carry-forwards onto one sheet; the self audit checklist does the accounts-and-subscriptions half of it.
  2. Decide the shape of 2027. The new year goals template runs eight life areas with a reason and a first step each; the word of the year worksheet picks the tiebreaker word; the SMART goals template and its two-page worksheet tighten a goal you already have.
  3. Put January on paper. The January reset checklist handles home, digital, money and calendar in one afternoon, and the monthly budget planner gives the money line somewhere to live.
  4. Carry the weeks. The weekly planner, the paper daily planner and the daily focus planner run the days; the 66 day habit tracker, the 90 day challenge tracker and the weekly goals template hold the streak; the quarterly life audit is the check-in that keeps February's list honest in May.

Print setup

Every page on this site is designed to print from a browser with no plugin: Ctrl+P or Cmd+P, A4 or US Letter, grayscale, margins default or none where a table runs wide. On the free worksheets the print button hides the navigation and the store links. If you are picking one page to start, print the yearly review tonight and the goals template this week; the review is the step people skip, and it is the one that decides whether the goal page says anything true.
