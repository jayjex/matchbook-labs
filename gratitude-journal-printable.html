Free Gratitude Journal Printable — Daily Page

One page you print and fill in by hand: three good things, one person to thank, a mood box, five reflection lines. Below it, the first ten days of a 30-day gratitude challenge and four weekly check-in questions. Nothing to install, no app, no account.

How to use it: print one page per day, or keep a small stack on the nightstand and fill it before bed. The whole page takes about five minutes. The lines are write-in on purpose; there is no right answer to grade.

Works on A4 and US Letter. If you miss a day, start again the next day; the streak matters less than coming back.

The daily gratitude page

Date   Day  

Three good things today

Small counts. A warm cup, a parking spot, a text you didn't expect.

Someone to thank

One name, and why. If you can, tell them before the week ends.

Mood today — circle one

1  2  3  4  5
1 = heavy, 5 = light. There is no wrong number; the pattern shows up over weeks, not days.

Reflection — five lines

Nothing on the page assumes a tradition or a translation, so it fits whatever you already read or practice. If you want a page with a prayer focus and a verse write-in slot too, that version lives on the prayer journal template.

30-day gratitude challenge — the first 10 days

One small prompt a day. Write the date next to it when you fill it; a missed day is just a day. These are days 1 through 10 of the 30 in the full pack.

01three gifts from today
02a person you are glad for
03a meal you enjoyed
04a skill you have
05something in nature
06a memory that makes you smile
07a book, song or film
08one corner of your home
09a stranger's kindness
10your body, one part that worked

Copy this into a notebook if you don't want to print the page. The prompts work in any order; the numbers just keep count. If you like tracking a run of days, the 90-day challenge tracker uses the same come-back-daily habit.

Weekly reflection — four questions

Once a week, look back over your pages and answer these four. Short answers are fine; one line each.

1Three moments that stayed.
2Prayers answered, or moving.
3A verse that held this week.
4What I am carrying into next week.

Question 2 is the one worth rereading. Entries that sat open for a month tend to close when you stop watching them.

Print tips

  • Print the daily page alone for a small stack, or double-sided with the challenge list on the back.
  • Grayscale prints fine; the layout is line-based, no ink-heavy fills.
  • Prefer typing? The three sections transfer into any document as headings and blank lines.
