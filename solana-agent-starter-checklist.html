Pre-build checklist

Four opinions from building payment rails

These are opinions, stated as opinions. They come from our ideathon work on the Matchbook pattern (Superteam Ukraine / Colosseum, September 2026), so read them as field notes from one build.

  • Test on devnet until it's boring. Excitement on devnet means bugs on mainnet. Boring means you're done.
  • Memos are not receipts. Wallets strip them and nothing on-chain binds them. If your agent's bookkeeping depends on a memo, it depends on a suggestion.
  • Enforce at settlement. A post-hoc parser reads history after it happens; a transfer hook decides whether it happens. A convention only holds when everyone follows it, and the transfers that matter are the ones where someone didn't.
  • Write the matcher before the frontend. Reconciliation is where payment projects stall. A clean UI over a broken ledger match is still broken.

Common mistakes (the honest list)

Committing secrets. The keypair lands in git history, scrapers find it within hours, and the wallet drains. Rotate the key, gitignore the file, move the secret to env vars.

Skipping simulation. Un-simulated transactions fail at settlement and pay fees for the privilege. Simulate first, send second.

Ignoring compute limits. The hook, its CPIs, and the PDA asserts all draw from the same CU budget. Code that passes unit tests can still fail mid-transfer on-chain.
