Weekly Review Template (Free, One Page)

A week ends whether you look at it or not. This weekly review template gives the look one page: 2 write-in rows for wins, 2 for misses, 2 for lessons, 2 for next week's priorities, and one 30-minute block to run it all in. Print free, fill it by hand, keep the page. No software, no account.

How it works: eight rows, two per section, one line each. No essays. Set the block line first so the review has a time to live in, then fill the four sections top to bottom. Monday starts with the two priorities already picked, before the inbox gets a vote.

Four opinions from running weekly reviews

  1. (Opinion.) Friday afternoon beats Sunday night for most weeks. The workspace this sheet borrows from runs its routine on Sunday, "30 minutes, same time every week". Both days work. Friday has one advantage: Friday-you still remembers why the misses happened, while Sunday-you only sees that they did.
  2. (Opinion.) Two rows per section is the feature, not the limit. A review that fits on one page gets done every week. A review that can grow into an essay gets skipped by week three. If the wins do not fit, keep the two you would want to read again in a year.
  3. (Opinion.) Write misses as facts with dates. "Proposal sent two days late" has a next action. "I keep procrastinating" has none. Verdicts feel honest and change nothing; a dated fact can be fixed or repeated on purpose.
  4. (Opinion.) The two priorities are a handoff, not a wish list. If both rows look equally important, neither is. Pick one, let the second row be the thing you do when the first one hits a wall.
