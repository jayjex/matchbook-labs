Free Prayer Journal Template — Printable Daily Page

One page you print and fill in by hand: three gratitude lines, one prayer focus, a verse to copy out, six reflection lines. Below it, a tracker for answered prayers and a log for verses you're memorizing. Nothing to install, no app, no account.

How to use it: print the page once per day, or once per sitting if daily is too much. The boxes are write-in on purpose. The verse slot takes whatever you're reading, in whatever wording you use; the page stays out of it.

Works on A4 and US Letter. For a reusable binder copy, print one and trace the layout onto cardstock.

Five starter prompts

For days when you sit down and nothing comes. Pick one, write the answer on the reflection lines.

1Write three gifts from today, however small they feel. A parking spot counts.
2Name the decision you keep putting off, in one plain sentence.
3List five ordinary things you used today without noticing them.
4What would you say to a friend carrying the exact worry you're carrying?
5Write the thank-you you never sent. Say the whole thing here.

Print tips

  • Print the daily page alone (page 1) and the trackers double-sided.
  • Grayscale prints fine; the layout is line-based, no ink-heavy fills.
  • Copy the tables into a spreadsheet instead if you'd rather type. The columns transfer as-is.
