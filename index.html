FAQ

What format do the products come in?

Instant digital download. Depending on the product: CSV, Markdown, JSON, HTML, PNG images, or a zip bundle with a README. No shipping, nothing physical. Each product page lists its exact files.

What can I do with the files? What's the license?

Personal and commercial use is allowed, including client work. You cannot resell or redistribute the files as-is or with minor changes. Data products can be used inside your own research, tooling, or reports.

Do you offer refunds or updates?

These are digital goods, so there are no refunds once the files are downloaded. Updates are free: if a product gets revised, you can re-download the latest version from your order page at no extra cost.

How do I pay?

On Getly, checkout runs on crypto. On Gumroad, you can pay by card or PayPal.

How do I get support?

Email orbitalstack88@uberip.com with your order ID and product name. Questions about files, imports, or broken links get answered within 24-48 hours.

Where does the data come from?

Public sources only: live APIs, official program pages, company career pages, and job boards. Everything is collected and HTTP-checked on a stated data date printed on each product page (for example, 2026-09-05). Data ages, so each product tells you its collection date. Nothing in the store is financial advice.
