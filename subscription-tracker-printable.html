Subscription Tracker, Free to Print

Every recurring charge you agreed to, on two pages. This subscription tracker printable is a monthly subscription list template you can print free: page 1 holds 12 write-in rows, one per service, each with a cost line, a renewal day to circle, and a keep/cancel circle. Page 2 turns the same list into an annual-cost recap, runs an unused-service check, and leaves 4 lines for cancellation notes. The sheet records; the circles stay yours.

How to use this page: print both pages at 100% scale (the site chrome drops out on paper). Fill page 1 one service at a time, from the statement or the app's subscription page, not from memory: write the service, write the cost, circle the day it renews. Leave the keep/cancel circle for after page 2, where the annual recap and the unused check give that circle something to stand on. Reprint page 1 when 12 rows run out.

Four opinions on running a subscription list

  1. (Opinion.) Fill the renewal circle the day after you sign up. The confirmation email is still at the top of the inbox, the renewal terms are one scroll away, and the circle takes ten seconds. A renewal day written weeks later gets guessed from memory, and a guessed day misses the one morning it matters.
  2. (Opinion.) Do the annual-cost recap before filling any keep/cancel circle. A monthly cost reads small because it is a monthly number; the recap multiplies it into the year, and the year is what the keep-or-cancel circle is about. Deciding from the monthly figure skips the only row built for the decision.
  3. (Opinion.) Run the unused check on the same day each month. Charges follow a schedule, so usage checks should too: three lines on page 2, same day, same pen. A check that happens whenever you remember happens rarely, and the services you forgot about stay forgotten.
  4. (Opinion.) Write the cancellation steps while the account is still open. Portal, email, or phone differs per service, and the steps are cheapest to write the moment you are logged in. The 4 lines on page 2 exist so the decision and the doing land on the same sheet.

What this page is

A subscription list, a renewal calendar, and a place for the calls you make about both. It records services, costs as your statements print them, renewal days, and the keep-or-cancel circles you fill; it predicts nothing, prices nothing, and recommends nothing. Nothing on this page is financial advice. What the sheet does is keep the whole list in one visible place, so the next renewal on it is a charge you looked at on purpose.
