Four opinions on organizing

These are opinions, not rules. Disagree with specifics.

  1. Opinion: book the venue before picking the topic. Capacity, date, and access rules shape the format more than the topic does. Topics are cheap to swap; venues on your date usually aren't available to swap at all.
  2. Opinion: plan the room at two-thirds of your yes count. Some people who RSVP don't show, so a packed-looking number is the wrong number to plan chairs, snacks, and seats around.
  3. Opinion: put a name on each box. The venue box gets one person's name, the supplies box gets another. A box owned by everyone is checked by nobody.
  4. Opinion: end on the hour you promised. People arrange rides, sitters, and mornings around your end time. Overrun once and the regulars weigh that the next time they decide to come.
