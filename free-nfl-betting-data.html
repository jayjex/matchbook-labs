Reading the Key Columns

  • spread_line - the closing spread, relative to the home team. Positive = home favored, negative = away favored. A favorite covers when its winning margin beats the absolute value of the line, and if the margin lands exactly on an integer line, every spread bet is a push.
  • total_line - the closing over/under on combined score. A total_line of 47.5 means the over cashes when the two teams combine for 48 or more points.
  • result and total - the actual outcome: result is home_score minus away_score, total is home_score plus away_score. Comparing those two columns against the closing lines is what makes ATS and over/under backtests possible.
  • away_moneyline / home_moneyline - closing moneylines in American odds. A negative number is what you'd risk to win $100; a positive number is the profit on a $100 bet. These are empty before 2006 because the source doesn't carry them for the old era. Same story for the odds columns attached to spreads and totals (away_spread_odds, home_spread_odds, under_odds, over_odds).

The sample keeps all 46 columns of the master file, including rest days, roof, surface, temperature, wind, both starting QBs, both head coaches, referee, and stadium.
