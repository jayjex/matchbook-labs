Free Printable Halloween Party Games

Three complete games on this page: a 20-question Would You Rather, a 10-word word scramble, and 10 emoji pictionary puzzles. Every answer is included. Hit Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P on Mac) and you have your party entertainment. No download, no email address, no sign-up. Hosting again in November? How to use this page: print it (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P), or read the questions off your phone and keep score on paper. The answer keys are collapsed below each game — print mode hides nothing you need, but the answer sections stay visible so the host can keep them on a second sheet.

Game 1 — Would You Rather: Halloween Edition

Read each pair aloud, count the raised hands, and argue about the weird ones. Works best after the second round of drinks. 20 questions, roughly 15 minutes.

  1. Have a vampire for a roommate or a ghost living in your car?
  2. Eat candy corn with every meal for a week, or drink pumpkin spice everything for a month?
  3. Spend one night alone in a haunted house, or one night in an abandoned shopping mall?
  4. Fight one wolf-sized bat, or a hundred bat-sized wolves?
  5. Only watch horror movies for a full year, or only watch nature documentaries?
  6. Go trick-or-treating as an adult with no costume, or attend a costume party where nobody else dressed up?
  7. Have permanent vampire fangs, or permanent werewolf hearing?
  8. Bob for apples in a kiddie pool of gravy, or eat a whole raw onion like an apple?
  9. Know the exact date of your death, or the cause but not the date?
  10. Carve 100 pumpkins in one day, or build full haunted-yard displays for 10 houses?
  11. Be haunted by a polite ghost, or stalked by a friendly vampire?
  12. Dress as a pumpkin every Halloween for the rest of your life, or never wear a costume again?
  13. Read a cursed book that gives you one month of bad luck, or wear a cursed mask that forces you to tell the truth for one month?
  14. Swap bodies with a zombie for a day, or with a house cat for a day?
  15. Your house gets toilet-papered every Halloween, or your car gets egged every Friday the 13th?
  16. Drink a witch's potion that makes you float for an hour, or one that makes you invisible for an hour?
  17. Spend Halloween in Salem, Massachusetts, or in a castle in Transylvania?
  18. Have candy that never runs out but tastes like cardboard, or candy that tastes incredible but you only get one piece per year?
  19. Sit through a 10-hour horror movie marathon, or a 10-hour true-crime podcast recorded live in your living room?
  20. Turn into a bat every sunrise, or a werewolf every full moon?
