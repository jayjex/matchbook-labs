Etsy Tags Optimizer Guide

How many tags an Etsy listing takes: 13, and each one caps at 20 characters. That is the whole spec, and it fits in one small table. The hard part is choosing phrases worth the slots, so this page pairs the numbers with a 6-step checklist, four opinions, and a look at a prompt pack that drafts tag sets for you.

How to use it: check your current tags against the facts table first, then run the checklist on one listing end to end. It takes about 15 minutes per listing. The four tips at the end are opinions and labeled as such; the table is not.

4 opinion tips

  1. Long-tail beats single word. (Opinion.) A specific phrase like "crossback linen apron" competes against fewer listings than "apron" does, and the buyer who types it knows what they want. I would rather match fewer searches that convert than a flood that browses.
  2. Put your strongest phrase in the title too. (Opinion.) Tags and titles are separate fields, and I treat them as one decision: the phrase worth a tag slot is usually worth the title's opening. Doing both keeps the listing speaking with one voice.
  3. Skip adjectives no buyer types. (Opinion.) "Cute," "beautiful," "amazing" eat characters without describing anything checkable. A tag that says "walnut serving tray" carries weight that "gorgeous tray" cannot, because one is a fact and the other is a mood.
  4. Retire duplicates before adding anything new. (Opinion.) When two tags differ by one word, one of them is dead weight. I would cut the weaker and use the slot on a phrase with a different intent behind it.
