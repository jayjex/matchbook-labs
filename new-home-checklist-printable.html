New Home Checklist Printable (8 Weeks to Move Day)

Move chaos comes from one thing: the tasks that only work 6 weeks out, discovered 2 days out. This checklist runs the other way. It is a countdown from 8 weeks out to move day, 41 items grouped by stage, followed by the top 15 address changes, a utility setup grid, and a first-week essentials box. Print it, stick it on the fridge, work top to bottom. The full 30-page New Home Binder keeps going after move day, linked at the bottom.

How to use this page: hit print, write your move date at the top, and start at 8 weeks out. Each stage is a gate: finish it before the next one starts, because the expensive tasks (movers, insurance, internet install) all have lead times. Skipping to the bottom is how people end up moving without locks changed or power on.

The move countdown, 8 weeks to move day

8 weeks out

  • Set the move budget: deposits, movers or truck rental, time off work, overlap on rent or mortgage
  • Book movers or reserve the truck (end-of-month slots go first)
  • Start the sell-or-give-away pile; whatever has not sold by move week gets donated
  • Order packing supplies: boxes, tape, markers, packing paper
  • Start the address change list (the 15 below)

6 weeks out

  • Book home or renters insurance for the new address, effective move-in day
  • Schedule utility turn-on and pay deposits (grid below)
  • Collect records: school, medical, dental, vet
  • Plan meals around freezer and pantry food; use it up instead of moving it
  • Renting? Give written notice and schedule the walkthrough

4 weeks out

  • Book move day and the day after off work
  • File the USPS mail forward, start date = move day
  • Pack the rooms you use least; label every box with room + contents
  • Arrange kids and pets care for move day
  • Measure the new place: door widths, hallway turns, big-furniture paths
  • Get utility turn-on dates confirmed in writing

2 weeks out

  • Reconfirm the movers; save a direct phone number for the crew
  • Book the internet install (2-week lead is normal; do it now)
  • Back up computers; bag and label cables
  • Confirm truck parking at both addresses
  • Return borrowed items, library books; cancel local memberships
  • Keep one cleaning kit out of the boxes on purpose

1 week out

  • Pack everything except the essentials box (below)
  • Defrost the freezer; disconnect the washer or book whoever does
  • Confirm keys: when, where, and from whom
  • Reserve the elevator or loading dock (apartments)
  • Set aside cleaning supplies, tools, lightbulbs, and toilet paper to ride in your car
  • Clean each room as its boxes leave; the final clean is half done

2 days out

  • Seal and stack boxes by the door, labeled side out
  • Load the essentials box last, into your car, not the truck
  • Confirm the arrival window with the movers
  • Photograph the back of the TV and router before unplugging anything

Move day

  • Final walk of the old place: closets, dishwasher, attic, behind every door
  • Photograph meters at both places, same day
  • Direct boxes room by room; tick each label off as it lands
  • Make the beds first, before the rest of the unpacking
  • Test smoke and CO alarms tonight
  • Re-key or change the locks; note where the water and gas shutoffs are
  • Find the breaker panel; relabel whatever the last owner guessed at
  • Hand over old keys per your agreement, then photograph them gone
  • Note damage or missing boxes before signing the mover's paperwork

41 items. Renting or buying, both work: renting skips the insurance-for-closing line, buying adds a closing-docs folder to the essentials box.

Top 15 address changes

Miss these and mail, paychecks, and insurance cards go to strangers. The USPS forward catches some of it for a year; the list below is the permanent fix:

  • USPS mail forward (start date = move day; lasts 12 months for first-class)
  • Employer and payroll, plus HR for insurance mailing addresses
  • Bank and credit cards (statements, new-card mailings, fraud letters)
  • Insurance policies: health, auto, home or renters, life
  • Phone carrier
  • DMV: license and vehicle registration (states set their own deadline)
  • Voter registration
  • Doctors, dentist, vet, pharmacy
  • Kids' school and activity programs
  • Tax agencies: IRS change of address (Form 8822) plus your state
  • Subscriptions and delivery services
  • Online shopping default addresses (remove the old one, don't only add the new)
  • Investment, retirement, and benefits accounts
  • Family and friends (one group text beats a year of "did you get my card?")
  • Gym and local memberships (cancel or transfer; some contracts want notice in writing)

First-week essentials box

Everything here rides in your car, never the truck. One box per person plus one shared box, or one big one for a small household:

  • Toiletries, shower curtain, towels
  • Toilet paper (every toilet in the new place, day one)
  • Sheets for every bed, one set each
  • Chargers, power strip, phone cables
  • Medications, glasses, first-aid basics
  • Box cutter, scissors, trash bags
  • Basic tools, lightbulbs, batteries
  • Paper towels and all-purpose cleaner
  • Coffee setup, snacks, water bottles
  • One pot, one pan, plates and forks for the crew
  • Documents folder: lease or closing papers, IDs, insurance cards
  • Kids' and pets' comfort items (the thing that makes the first night normal)
  • Nightlight for unfamiliar hallways

Common questions

What if I have 2 weeks instead of 8?

Run the same stages as days: budget and movers on day 1, insurance and utilities on day 2, packing starts day 3. Two compressions matter: book movers before anything else (availability, not price, is the constraint in a short window), and cut possessions harder, because packing labor is what runs out first. The countdown order stays the same; only the calendar shrinks.

When should I turn utilities on if closing and move-in are the same weekend?

Set turn-on for the morning of closing day, and keep the old place on through the day after move-out. Utilities overlap costs less than a weekend without water or power, and buyers doing their own walk-through will want the power on for inspections anyway.

How early can I file the address change with USPS?

Up to 3 months before the start date, and you can edit or cancel the request online with the confirmation code. Filing early at 4 weeks out removes one whole errand from move week.
