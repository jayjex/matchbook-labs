Deadline hygiene

Track submission cutoffs, not event end dates. Many hackathons list a final day, but the project submission form closes earlier, and some close a full day before the closing ceremony. Write the actual form-closing time from the event page, then convert it to your timezone and put both on the sheet. Timezones are where most people slip: a "23:59 deadline" is 23:59 somewhere, and if that somewhere is UTC, an evening in Jakarta or a morning in New York can already be too late. Check the deadline the day before, not the day of, because organizers do move cutoffs and announce it in Discord channels you may not have open.

Prep checklist — do these before kickoff

  • Team locked. Confirm who's building before the event starts. "We'll find a designer on day one" is how solo stacks happen.
  • Repo ready. Create the repository, push a README skeleton, and make sure everyone can clone and commit on day one.
  • Demo note. One page, written before you code: what the demo shows, in what order, in 90 seconds. This becomes your submission video script.
  • Environment set. Wallet, testnet funds, API keys, node access — set up before kickoff, not during.
  • Track picked. Choose your prize track early and read its judging criteria. Build toward what the judges score.
  • Submission form drafted. Open the Devpost (or equivalent) form on day one and fill in what you can. Fields you draft early are fields you don't rush at the cutoff.

4 tips for first-time hackers

Opinions, from watching a lot of submissions land:

  1. Scope small. One feature that works end-to-end beats three that almost work. Judges can only score what runs.
  2. Sleep at least once. All-nighters feel committed but they show up in the demo: wrong clicks, forgotten steps, dead air. A rested team demos better than a heroic one.
  3. Submit an ugly version early. Get a rough project in hours before the cutoff, then polish. A submitted 7/10 beats an unsubmitted 9/10, every time.
  4. Talk to mentors mid-event. Ask for feedback while you can still change direction, not in the last Q&A slot when it's too late to act on it.
