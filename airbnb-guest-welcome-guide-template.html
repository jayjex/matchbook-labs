Page 1: a note and the rules

Four opinions on the one-page guide

Opinions, labeled as such, take or leave:

  1. One page beats a PDF binder. (Opinion.) Arrival is the worst moment to hand someone a document. One flat sheet on the counter answers the first-hour questions; a file on a tablet assumes a charged tablet and a guest willing to scroll.
  2. Hang it where bags drop. (Opinion.) Kitchen counter, fridge door, next to the coffee maker. If the sheet needs a search, it won't get read.
  3. Five rules is the ceiling. (Opinion.) Everything past five lines turns into fine print, and fine print is easy to ignore. The long version belongs in the listing, where guests read it before booking.
  4. Date it in the corner. (Opinion.) Codes and passwords rotate. A small date tells you which copy is current and which one heads for the trash.
